## Changes

- Faster SD-card video playback on AMOLED and JC4827W543 through frame prefetch
  and queued display strips.
- ST7701 frame prefetch and a lower 12 MHz RGB pixel clock to reduce transient
  vertical screen shifts. Experimental strip batching and synchronized buffer
  waiting were removed after they reduced performance.
- C6 retains its simpler big-endian pixel-transfer improvement.
- Playback timing summaries help diagnose decoding, display and SD read costs.
- Cheap Yellow Display receives the current shared firmware; it does not enable
  the PSRAM-dependent prefetch or queued-strip paths.

Configure Wi-Fi through the app/USB. These are first-install firmware packages,
not OTA images. Use an app version supporting your exact board model, including
AMOLED where applicable. Existing published firmware releases remain available.

## Build provenance and validation

- The maintainer supplied fresh 1.1.38 builds for all five profiles and confirmed
  on 2026-09-28 that all are credential-free and ready for release.
- Accompanying ESPImageDisplay checkout: `6987b57c08566feb5d6e55fa85ccd257a2fa021b`,
  clean at packaging time. This identifies the checkout, not a cryptographic
  proof that the binaries were compiled from that revision.
- Exact profile/FQBN, toolchain/library package identities and binary hashes:
  [build provenance](https://github.com/thelastoutpostworkshop/ESPImageFirmware/blob/v1.1.38/releases/v1.1.38-builds.json).
- All application binaries contain 1.1.38 and the expected board identifier;
  supplied sketch dependency files do not reference `secrets.h`. Dependency
  inspection alone does not prove a binary contains no credentials.
- Packages validated for hashes, chip headers, offsets, flash capacity and
  partition fit, then extracted and accepted by the desktop package validator.
- No compilation, flashing or new physical-board tests were performed during
  packaging. Hardware readiness is the maintainer's confirmation. Recorded
  playback tests during development include approximately 15 FPS on AMOLED/JC,
  14 FPS on C6, and 14.3-14.7 FPS with no observed vertical shifts on ST7701
  1.1.38. These figures depend on the clip and are not guaranteed frame rates.
- Cheap Yellow Display playback was visually reviewed; the reef clip's quality
  limitations include source content, resizing and JPEG compression. No new
  conversion-quality change is included in this firmware release.
